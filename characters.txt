Jay Gatsby - a yong, mysterious millionaire with shady business connections. Originally from North Dakota. During World War I, when he was a young military officer stationed at the United States Army's Camp Taylor in Louisville, Kentucky, he encountered the love of his life. the beautiful debutante Daisy Buchanan. He has one goal in life: to reunite with his true love Daisy Buchanan. He is fixated on repeating the past and winning back her love.
